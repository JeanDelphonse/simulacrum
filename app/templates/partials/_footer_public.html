{# The public site footer — one copy, shared by the landing page and every external page. Self-contained on purpose: landing.html is a standalone document with its own CSS, while the external pages come through base.html and main.css. A partial that carried no styles of its own would render differently on either side, so the colours are literal here rather than var() references, and every class is `pf-` prefixed. Without the prefix `.footer-tagline` would collide with the italic rule main.css already defines under that name. Product links point at the landing page's anchors, so they work from any page rather than only from the one that happens to contain those sections. #}
