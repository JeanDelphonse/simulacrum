Email Import (Email-to-CRM)

Users connect mailboxes and SimiSI imports the real people they correspond with into their CRM. A global off always wins. Turning it off for anyone pauses or revokes their connections and marks every contact it imported Do Not Contact straight away; turning it back on lifts nothing. Every change needs a reason and is logged.
