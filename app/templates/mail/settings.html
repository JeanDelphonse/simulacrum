Email import

Connect your email and SimiSI turns the real people you correspond with into contacts in My Contacts, each field labelled with where it came from. Spam, marketing and automated mail are skipped.

  • Read-only. SimiSI can't send, delete or change your mail.
  • The minimum. Message headers, and the signature block of the newest message from each person. Email bodies, attachments and signature text are never stored.
  • Nothing sends. Imported contacts wait in My Contacts for you; any outreach still needs your approval.
  • Your email data is never used to train models, sold, or shared with data brokers.
