How it works

From a plan on paper to proof a customer would sign

Four steps, at your pace. Every step builds on the one before, and nothing reaches a customer or investor without your approval.

1

Pitch

Upload your deck, plan or executive summary, or simply talk it through. Missing documents show as gaps, not as zeros.

2

Evaluate

A readiness report against a nine-part rubric. Every finding quotes the passage it comes from, so you can check it.

3

Build the team

Agents matched to your gaps. You decide how much each one does on its own, from advice only to acting within limits.

4

Execute

One clear next step at a time. Each piece of proof moves you up the evidence ladder, from a claim to a paying customer.

{# ── FAQ ── #}

Questions founders ask

Do I need a finished business plan?

No. Start with whatever you have: a deck, a few pages, or a spoken pitch. Anything missing shows up as a gap to fill, not as a failing score.

Will the agents contact people without asking me?

Not at the default settings. Agents draft; you approve. You choose how much each agent may do on its own, and some actions always wait for you.

Can my co-founders use it too?

Yes. Invite them to the venture. Each of you answers the alignment questions separately, and decision rights show who has the final say on what.

Does SimiSI help me raise money?

SimiSI helps you build and present evidence. It does not predict funding, promise investor interest or give legal or investment advice.
