Venture trace

Runs, plan-step changes, approvals and evaluations for one venture, newest first. Opening a trace is recorded in the access audit. Documents, contact names and message text are masked unless the founders turned on support access.

Choose a venture to see its trace.

External actions

Every drafted, approved, sent or blocked external action with the autonomy level it needed and the level it went out under. Levels: 1 advisor only, 2 approve each action, 3 approve the campaign, 4 act within limits. A send above its approved level is flagged at once; the expected count is zero.
