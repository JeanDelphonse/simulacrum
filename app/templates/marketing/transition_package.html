FAQ

Questions HR teams ask.

Can we see how individual employees are doing?

No. You see seat status for each person (invited, activated, expired), and everything else only in aggregate, for groups of five or more. Simulations, contacts, outreach and coaching notes are private to the employee.

How fast can employees start?

As soon as you upload the roster. Invites go to personal email addresses on the date you choose, and employees can run their simulation and publish their bio page the same day.

Do the agents send anything without the employee's approval?

No. Agents prepare drafts and plans. Anything sent, published or booked goes out from the employee's own account, only after they approve it.

What happens when the access period ends?

The employee keeps their account and bio page on SimiSI's free plan, and can choose to buy simulations themselves. Unused seats can be reassigned while your contract is active.

Is the package taxable to employees?

Employer-paid outplacement is often treated as a non-taxable benefit when it is provided based on need and isn't offered in place of cash severance. Confirm the treatment with your tax adviser.

Does SimiSI give financial advice?

No. The simulation includes planning guidance about wealth, but no part of the package is financial, legal or tax advice, and SimiSI never moves anyone's money.

How is it priced?

Per seat, by package, with volume pricing and annual seat pools. Request a quote and we'll respond within one business day.
