Why the layers run in order

You can't productize expertise you haven't learned to sell one-to-one, or invest money the first four layers haven't earned yet. SimiSI works through the layers in order, and a Bayesian orchestrator decides which agent should act next based on your results so far.

    Questions people ask before they start

    Anything else? Email simi@simulacrumai.io.

    What does the free bio page include?
    Your free bio page is written from your resume and includes a professional bio, an assistant that answers visitors' questions on your behalf, a lead capture form that saves contacts to your CRM, SEO meta tags, and three design themes. No credit card required, free forever.
    How does the assistant on my bio page work?
    When a visitor opens the chat on your bio page, they talk to an assistant that knows your resume, services and expertise. It answers questions about your background and availability, and captures their name and email into your CRM, even at 2 a.m. when you're not available.
    What is a simulation?
    A full run of your career-to-wealth engine. Our Bayesian orchestrator identifies your expertise zone and deploys {{ site_layers.agent_total }} specialized agents across {{ site_layers.count }} income layers, from active consulting (Layer 1) to wealth deployment (Layer 5). Each agent produces a specific deliverable: a rate card, a cold email campaign, a course outline, a blog post and more.
    How is the simulation priced?
    It is a one-time payment per simulation, not a subscription. {% if pricing.is_discounted %}The simulation is normally {{ pricing.original_price }}; with {{ pricing.discount_pct }}% launch pricing you pay {{ pricing.current_price }} once.{% elif pricing.available %}You pay {{ pricing.current_price }} once.{% else %}You pay once, at the price shown at checkout.{% endif %} You receive all {{ site_layers.agent_total }} deliverables and keep access to your results. A second simulation, on a different expertise zone or an updated resume, is a separate one-time purchase at the price shown at checkout.
    What happens to my data?
    Your resume and career data are processed only to generate your bio page and simulation. We never sell your data. You can delete your account and all its data at any time from Settings. Your visitors' contact details are stored in your private CRM and are never shared.
    What does each integration unlock?
    All integrations are optional; SimiSI works fully without connecting any tool. Each one lets your agents act through it: Apollo finds prospects to contact, ConvertKit publishes to your list, Cal.com sets up your booking page, Stripe creates payment links, and PandaDoc prepares proposals.{% if show_integrations %} See the full list.{% endif %} Your bio page and its assistant are live from the moment you upload your resume.
    Will SimiSI email people without my permission?
    Only on channels you allow. Outreach runs on your simulation's email channel, which is on by default so your campaign can start; switch it off and every message waits for your okay. A one-off send from an artifact always asks you to confirm the exact emails first, with an unsubscribe link in each. Anyone who opts out or is marked do-not-contact is never emailed.
    Does it use my own email account?
    Emails go out under your name, and replies come back to you. Where own-account sending is enabled, you connect your SendGrid account and a verified sender, and emails go out from your own domain. Payments, bookings and newsletters run through your own Stripe, Cal.com and ConvertKit accounts.
    Can agents move my money?
    No. Wealth-deployment agents advise only; they never place trades or move funds. A payment link on your Stripe collects money for you; nothing ever spends it.
    What if an agent makes a mistake?
    Every deliverable is yours to review and edit, and your edits are kept as versions that a re-run never overwrites. Actions like payment links and booking pages wait for your approval and can be undone, and every action is logged.
