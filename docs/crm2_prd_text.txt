Prospect Discovery Agent
Apollo Firmographic Search · Claude Fit-Scoring · Split Auto-Save · Acquisition/Size Guardrails · Review Queue
Document ID
SIM-PRD-CRM-002
Version
1.0
Extends
SIM-PRD-CRM-001 (Admin Outreach Pipeline). This adds automated discovery of new prospect firms into that pipeline. It does not change the pipeline, stages, or the semi-auto touch loop — it feeds them.
Route
/admin/contacts → Outreach → Discover. A discovery panel plus a review queue, admin-only.
What it does
Given a search profile (firmographic filters), the agent queries Apollo for matching companies, scores each with Claude for fit against Simulacrum's employer-pilot thesis, flags likely-acquired or too-large firms, and routes results: high-fit firms auto-save into the pipeline at 'Researched', everything else waits in a review queue for one-tap approval.
Why Apollo, not web scraping
There is no clean, permissible way to scrape web search results into a database, and search results are unstructured. Apollo returns structured firmographic data (name, domain, headcount, industry, location, leadership) and is already a platform-brokered integration (SIM-PRD-CONNECT-001). Web search is used only to ENRICH individual high-fit candidates, never as the discovery mechanism.
The core insight
Apollo can filter by size and industry but cannot judge FIT — whether a firm is one 'whose consultants' personal brand is the business and whose growth constraint is BD capacity.' That judgment is the Claude scoring layer, and it is what turns a raw company list into a qualified prospect list.
Connects to
SIM-PRD-CRM-001 (pipeline, stages, prospect record), SIM-PRD-CONNECT-001 (Apollo brokered), founder_ops.py (can run discovery on a schedule), the Corporate Partner sell sheet (defines the target thesis the scorer applies).

1. How Discovery Works
Apollo finds companies that match firmographic filters. Claude judges which of them fit the thesis. High-fit firms flow straight into the pipeline; the rest wait in a review queue. The founder approves the queue in seconds instead of researching firms for hours.
Step
Engine
What happens
1 Filter
Founder
Define or pick a search profile: category, headcount range, geography, keywords. Saved and reusable.
2 Search
Apollo
Query Apollo for companies matching the filters. Returns name, domain, headcount, industry, location, and leadership where available. Paginated; de-duplicated against existing prospects.
3 Score
Claude
Each company is scored high / medium / low for FIT against the employer-pilot thesis, with a one-line rationale. Firms that look acquired, too large, or off-category are flagged, not scored high.
4 Enrich
Web search
For high-fit candidates only, an optional enrichment pass finds a named founder/leader and a recent signal (funding, growth, press) to sharpen the first touch.
5 Route
System
High-fit → auto-saved into the pipeline at 'Researched' (so Touch 1 drafts immediately). Medium/low and flagged → the review queue for one-tap approve or dismiss.
2. The Search Profile
A saved set of firmographic filters passed to Apollo. The founder can keep several — one for fractional-exec firms, one for boutique management consultancies, one per geography.
Filter
Specification
Category / industry
Apollo industry tags plus keyword match: management consulting, HR consulting, fractional executive, advisory, boutique strategy. Multiple allowed.
Headcount range
The defining filter for this motion: default 5-25 employees. Below 5 is often a solo shop with no team to onboard; above 25 drifts out of the boutique band the pitch targets.
Geography
Optional. US-wide by default (the product is not location-bound); can narrow to a metro for a focused campaign.
Keywords
Positive keywords ('fractional', 'boutique', 'advisory', 'consultancy') and negative keywords ('staffing', 'recruiting', 'franchise') to steer Apollo and pre-filter noise.
Exclusions
Domains already in the pipeline, already onboarded, or previously passed are excluded automatically so discovery never re-surfaces a firm already handled.
3. The Claude Fit Scorer
This is the intelligence layer. Apollo returns companies of the right size and industry; only Claude can judge whether a firm fits the thesis that its consultants' business development would become the firm's pipeline. Without this step, discovery is just a company list. With it, discovery is a QUALIFIED prospect list.
What the scorer evaluates
Is this a firm where the consultants ARE the product — senior people selling their own expertise — rather than a staffing shop or a productized-service business?
Is business-development capacity plausibly the growth constraint (the pitch's core promise)?
Is it independent, or does the signal suggest it was acquired or is a subsidiary? Acquired firms are flagged, never scored high.
Is the headcount genuinely in the boutique band, or does Apollo's number look stale/mis-tagged?
Output per company
{
  company: 'The Boutique COO',
  domain: 'theboutiquecoo.com',
  headcount: 12,
  fit: 'high',                 # high | medium | low
  rationale: 'Fractional CEO/COO collective; senior operators
              selling their own expertise; BD capacity is the
              constraint. Strong thesis match.',
  flags: [],                   # e.g. ['possibly_acquired','too_large']
  suggested_category: 'Fractional Exec',
  route: 'auto_save'           # auto_save | review_queue
}
The scorer runs on Haiku for cost (this is a classification task, not generation), consistent with the model-routing approach in agents.json. A batch of 50 companies scores cheaply. The rationale is retained and shown in the pipeline so the founder always knows WHY a firm was surfaced.
4. Split Auto-Save & the Review Queue
Score / flag
Route
Behavior
High fit, no flags
Auto-save
Enters the pipeline at 'Researched' with fit=high, category, and rationale. Touch 1 drafts immediately in the founder-ops briefing. The founder sees them as new researched prospects, ready to send.
Medium / low fit
Review queue
Waits for the founder. One-tap Approve (enters at 'Researched') or Dismiss (recorded so it is never re-surfaced).
Flagged (acquired / too large / off-category)
Review queue, marked
Held with the flag visible ('possibly acquired — verify'), so a stale or wrong firm is caught BEFORE it costs a touch. Never auto-saved regardless of fit.
The acquisition/size guardrail is the feature that would have caught Blue Beyond (acquired by BDO, 2025) and Waterstone (acquired by West Monroe, 2019) before they reached the pipeline — the exact failures hit during manual research. A flagged firm never auto-saves, even at high apparent fit.
The founder controls the auto-save threshold: default is 'high fit only auto-saves.' It can be tightened (nothing auto-saves; review everything) or the batch size capped, so a large Apollo result set never floods the pipeline unreviewed.
5. Running Discovery
Mode
Specification
On demand
The founder picks a saved profile and clicks 'Discover'. Apollo query, scoring, and routing run; results land in the pipeline (auto-saved) and the review queue within the run. A progress state shows companies found / scored / routed.
Scheduled
A profile can run on a cadence (e.g. weekly) via the founder-ops job, so new firms matching the profile surface without the founder initiating. New results appear in the review queue and the morning briefing summarizes them ('6 new prospects auto-saved, 9 awaiting review').
Rate + cost control
Apollo calls respect the account's plan limits; batch size is capped per run (default 50). Scoring cost is bounded because it runs on Haiku. The founder sees an estimated Apollo credit cost before a large run.
Dedup + memory
Every discovered domain is remembered whether saved, queued, or dismissed, so re-running a profile never re-surfaces the same firm. Dismissed firms stay dismissed unless the founder clears them.
6. Data Model
CREATE TABLE discovery_profiles (
    id           CHAR(9) PRIMARY KEY,
    name         VARCHAR(120) NOT NULL,
    categories   JSON NULL,        -- industry tags + keywords
    headcount_min INT DEFAULT 5,
    headcount_max INT DEFAULT 25,
    geography    VARCHAR(160) NULL,
    keywords_pos JSON NULL,
    keywords_neg JSON NULL,
    auto_save_threshold VARCHAR(10) DEFAULT 'high',  -- high|none
    schedule     VARCHAR(20) NULL, -- null | weekly | monthly
    created_at   TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);
CREATE TABLE discovery_candidates (
    id            CHAR(9) PRIMARY KEY,
    profile_id    CHAR(9) NULL REFERENCES discovery_profiles(id),
    company       VARCHAR(200) NOT NULL,
    domain        VARCHAR(200) NOT NULL,
    headcount     INT NULL,
    industry      VARCHAR(120) NULL,
    location      VARCHAR(160) NULL,
    leader_name   VARCHAR(160) NULL,   -- from Apollo/enrichment
    fit           VARCHAR(10) NULL,    -- high|medium|low
    rationale     TEXT NULL,
    flags         JSON NULL,           -- possibly_acquired, too_large...
    route         VARCHAR(20) NULL,    -- auto_save|review_queue
    status        VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'queued',
                  -- queued | saved | dismissed
    prospect_id   CHAR(9) NULL,        -- set when saved (CRM-001 link)
    discovered_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
    UNIQUE KEY uq_domain (domain)      -- global dedup memory
);
-- Saving a candidate creates an admin_prospects row (CRM-001)
-- at stage 'researched', carrying fit, category, rationale.
7. Functional Requirements
Discovery
FR-DSC-01  A Discover panel in the Outreach tab lets the founder define and save reusable search profiles (categories/keywords, headcount range default 5-25, geography, positive/negative keywords). Profiles run on demand or on a schedule via founder-ops.
FR-DSC-02  Discovery queries Apollo (platform-brokered) for matching companies, returning name, domain, headcount, industry, location, and leadership where available. Results are de-duplicated against existing prospects and previously discovered domains (uq_domain), so a firm is never surfaced twice.
Scoring & guardrails
FR-DSC-03  Each company is scored high/medium/low for fit against the employer-pilot thesis by Claude (Haiku), with a retained one-line rationale. The rationale is shown wherever the prospect appears.
FR-DSC-04  Firms exhibiting acquisition, subsidiary, oversize, or off-category signals are flagged and NEVER auto-saved regardless of apparent fit. Flags are shown in the review queue ('possibly acquired — verify').
Routing
FR-DSC-05  Routing: high-fit unflagged firms auto-save into the CRM-001 pipeline at stage 'Researched' (Touch 1 drafts immediately); medium/low and all flagged firms go to a review queue for one-tap Approve (enter at 'Researched') or Dismiss (remembered, never re-surfaced). The auto-save threshold is founder-configurable, defaulting to high-only; it can be set so nothing auto-saves.
FR-DSC-06  High-fit candidates may be enriched via a web-search pass for a named leader and recent signal before entering the pipeline. Enrichment is best-effort and never blocks saving.
FR-DSC-07  Batch size is capped per run (default 50) and Apollo usage respects plan limits; the founder sees an estimated credit cost before a large run. Scheduled runs summarize results in the morning briefing (auto-saved count and awaiting-review count).
